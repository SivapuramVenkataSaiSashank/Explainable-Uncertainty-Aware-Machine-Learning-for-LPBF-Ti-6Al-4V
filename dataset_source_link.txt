3. Dataset Contents & Structure:
   - Process Parameters:
     * Laser Power: 100 W to 400 W
     * Scan Speed: 400 mm/s to 1600 mm/s
     * Hatch Spacing: 105 um (0.105 mm)
     * Layer Thickness: 30 um (0.030 mm)
     * Energy Density Metrics: Linear Energy Density (LED), Volumetric Energy Density (VED)
   - Structure & Defect Metrics:
     * X-ray Computed Tomography (XCT) internal defect volume fraction (porosity %)
     * Pore morphology distributions: equivalent diameter, sphericity, aspect ratio, projected area
     * Surface roughness: Ra, RMS (Rq) via optical surface profilometry
     * Microstructure: Prior-beta and alpha/alpha' grain morphology via EBSD / SEM
   - Mechanical Properties:
     * Microhardness (Vickers HV0.5)
     * Tensile properties: Yield Strength (YS), Ultimate Tensile Strength (UTS), Elongation at Failure (Elongation %)

4. Project Curated Datasets Included in This Submission:
   - lpbf_ti6al4v_psp_dataset.csv: Cleaned, consolidated 28-condition multi-level dataset
   - lpbf_ti6al4v_data_dictionary.csv: Complete data dictionary with variable definitions, units, and categories
   - lpbf_raw_pore_aggregated.csv: Aggregated statistics from 10,000+ raw individual XCT pore records
   - model_benchmark_results.csv: Comprehensive 5-fold CV evaluation metrics across Ridge, RF, GPR, XGBoost, and PyTorch MLP
   - validation_leakage_comparison.csv: Empirical comparison of random CV vs. process-grouped CV demonstrating data leakage
   - pareto_optimal_conditions.csv: Multi-objective Pareto frontier conditions balancing productivity, density, and strength
